# What is CST (Central Standard Time)?

**CST — Central Standard Time** is the standard time for the Central time zone in the United States during the non-daylight-saving months. It is **6 hours behind Coordinated Universal Time (UTC−6)**.

If it's noon in London (UTC/GMT), it's 6:00 am in Chicago. If it's noon in New York (EST, UTC−5), it's 11:00 am in Chicago.

CST is the "winter" version of Central time. During daylight saving time (mid-March to early November), the same places switch to **CDT — Central Daylight Time**, which is UTC−5.

## Where is CST used?

CST covers a huge swath of central North America. In the United States, it includes 9 states fully (AL, AR, IL, IA, LA, MN, MS, MO, WI) plus parts of Kansas, Kentucky, North Dakota, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, and Nebraska.

In Canada, CST is used in Manitoba, Saskatchewan (year-round, no DST), and parts of Nunavut. Mexico's capital, Mexico City, uses CST in winter (most of Mexico follows US DST rules).

## CST vs CDT — what's the difference?

Same Central time zone, different clock reading. CST is the **standard** time, used in winter. CDT is the **daylight saving** time, used in spring and summer. The difference is exactly 1 hour.

| Season | Abbreviation | UTC offset | Dates (2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Winter (standard) | CST | UTC−6 | Jan 1 – Mar 7, Nov 1 – Dec 31 |
| Summer (daylight) | CDT | UTC−5 | Mar 8 – Oct 31 |

**Note:** Most of Saskatchewan, Canada stays on CST year-round and does not observe CDT. The city of Lloydminster, on the Alberta-Saskatchewan border, also stays on CST year-round.

## CST compared to other US time zones

- **1 hour behind EST** (Eastern Standard Time, UTC−5)
- **1 hour ahead of MST** (Mountain Standard Time, UTC−7)
- **2 hours ahead of PST** (Pacific Standard Time, UTC−8)
- **3 hours ahead of AKST** (Alaska Standard Time, UTC−9)
- **4 hours ahead of HST** (Hawaii Standard Time, UTC−10)

## History of Central Standard Time

The Central time zone was created on November 18, 1883, when North American railroads adopted a standard time system ("Standard Railway Time"). Chicago was a natural hub for Central time because it was the railroad center of the US Midwest.

In 1918, the Standard Time Act made time zones federal law. Mexico followed with its own time zone system in 1922, adopting the same UTC offsets as the US for practical reasons.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: What does CST stand for?**
A: Central Standard Time. It refers to the standard (non-DST) time for the Central time zone in North America.

**Q: Is CST the same as Chicago time?**
A: Only in winter. Chicago observes CDT (UTC−5) in summer and CST (UTC−6) in winter.

**Q: What is the difference between CST and EST?**
A: EST is 1 hour ahead of CST. When it's noon EST (New York), it's 11:00 am CST (Chicago).

**Q: Which US states use CST?**
A: 9 states fully (AL, AR, IL, IA, LA, MN, MS, MO, WI) plus parts of KS, KY, ND, OK, SD, TN, TX, NE.

**Q: When does CST change to CDT in 2026?**
A: At 2:00 am local time on Sunday, March 8, 2026, clocks spring forward to CDT (UTC−5). They fall back to CST on Sunday, November 1, 2026.
