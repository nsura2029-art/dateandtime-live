About Pacific Standard Time

PST — Pacific Standard Time is the standard time for the Pacific time zone in the United States. It is 8 hours behind Coordinated Universal Time (UTC−8).

If it's noon in London (UTC/GMT), it's 4:00 am in Los Angeles. If it's noon in New York (EST, UTC−5), it's 9:00 am in Los Angeles.

PST is the "winter" version of Pacific time. During daylight saving time (mid-March to early November), the same places switch to PDT — Pacific Daylight Time (UTC−7).

Where is PST used?

PST covers 5 US states fully or mostly (CA, WA, OR, NV, ID-north). In Canada, PST is used in British Columbia (in winter), Yukon (year-round, no DST), and parts of BC. It is also the standard time in some parts of Mexico (Baja California).

Note on Yukon: Yukon Territory in Canada stays on PST (technically UTC−7, called "MST" historically) year-round and does not observe DST.

The DST transition happens at 2:00 am local time. Spring forward jumps from 2:00 am to 3:00 am. Fall back goes from 2:00 am to 1:00 am.

PST compared to other US time zones

  • 3 hours behind EST (Eastern Standard Time, UTC−5)
  • 2 hours behind CST (Central Standard Time, UTC−6)
  • 1 hour behind MST (Mountain Standard Time, UTC−7)
  • 1 hour ahead of AKST (Alaska Standard Time, UTC−9)
  • 2 hours ahead of HST (Hawaii Standard Time, UTC−10)

History of Pacific Standard Time

The Pacific time zone was created on November 18, 1883, with the adoption of Standard Railway Time. The zone was drawn based on the 120th meridian west, which passes through the Pacific Northwest.

In 1949, the California Legislature briefly voted to stay on PDT year-round ("War Time"), but the measure was repealed in 1950 after public backlash. California voters have since passed propositions (2018's Proposition 7) to make DST permanent if federal law changes.

Frequently asked questions

Q: What does PST stand for? A: Pacific Standard Time. It refers to the standard (non-DST) time for the Pacific time zone in North America.

Q: Is PST the same as Los Angeles time? A: Only in winter. Los Angeles observes PDT (UTC−7) in summer and PST (UTC−8) in winter.

Q: What is the difference between PST and EST? A: 3 hours. EST is 3 hours ahead of PST. When it's noon EST (New York), it's 9:00 am PST (Los Angeles).

Q: Which US states use PST? A: 5 states fully or mostly: CA, WA, OR, NV, ID (northern part).

Q: When does PST change to PDT in 2026? A: At 2:00 am local time on Sunday, March 8, 2026, clocks spring forward to PDT (UTC−7). They fall back to PST on Sunday, November 1, 2026.
