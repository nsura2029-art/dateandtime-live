3. API readiness — what's there, what's missing

EndpointReturnsStatusWhat we use it for
GET /api/v1/timezones All 400 IANA timezones with current abbr, offset, DST flag LIVE Hub page, dropdown, country maps
GET /api/v1/timezones/:id Single timezone detail (lat, lon, countries, offset) LIVE TZ detail page hero, sidebar widgets
GET /api/v1/dst/upcoming?tz=... Only NEXT 1 transition (date + offset before/after) PARTIAL DST countdown. We need 5 past + 5 future for the history table.
GET /api/v1/time/sun?lat&lon&date Sunrise + sunset only. No direction, no altitude, no day length. PARTIAL Sun cards. We need direction (° from N), altitude, day length, sun distance.
GET /api/v1/cities/:id City core: tz, lat/lon, pop, country, state, IANA LIVE City hero, all city pages
GET /api/v1/cities/:id/climate?month Climate summaries (12 months, hi/lo, precipitation) LIVE Climate year-round section
GET /api/v1/cities/:id/airports?limit=5 5 nearest airports with IATA, size, distance LIVE Airports section
GET /api/v1/cities/:id/aliases Historical city name redirects (Bombay→Mumbai) LIVE SEO 301 redirects for old city names
GET /api/v1/holidays/today?country Today's holidays for a country LIVE Today bar, country page
GET /api/v1/holidays/upcoming?country&days Upcoming holidays (next N days) LIVE Today bar, city page
GET /api/v1/onthisday?month&day Historical events for today LIVE Today bar, city page "On this day"
GET /api/v1/countries/:cca2/working-hours Business calendar: work hours, work days LIVE Meeting planner
Moon phase, rise/set — MISSING Moon section. Need compute from ephemeris or use suncalc.js client-side
Tides (high/low) — MISSING Tides section. NOAA API for US coastal. Non-US: no good public source.
Weather (full) — MISSING Weather section. Currently use Open-Meteo client-side. Could be server-side via API.
GET /api/v1/cities/:id/nearby 5 nearest cities with distance MISSING Cities near section
GET /api/v1/cities/:id/news News articles for country MISSING News section (currently static manifest)
Bottom line: 11/18 APIs are live, 2 are partial (DST + sun), 5 are missing (moon, tides, weather-full, nearby, news-per-city). The 5 missing aren't blockers for the educational TZ pages — those just need the 11 live APIs.

5. The educational TZ pages plan

timeanddate.com/time/zones/est has ~1,500 words covering: what is EST, who uses it, UTC offset, major cities, DST behavior, history, current time. We can match + beat this with our design system.

8. Article templates — what to put in each TZ page

Each TZ article has the same structure (~800-1,200 words + 5-6 data widgets). Let me sketch the template for EST as the canonical example:

EST page outline

  1. Hero (live) — "EST — Eastern Standard Time · UTC−5" with live clock + DST badge
  2. What is EST? (200 words) — Standard time for Eastern time zone during non-DST months. UTC offset 5 hours behind. Active in winter in 17 US states + parts of Canada + Caribbean + Central America.
  3. Where is EST used? — Map + list of US states (FL, NY, PA, GA, etc. excluding IN counties), Canadian provinces (ON, QC), Caribbean (Cuba, Haiti, Jamaica, Bahamas, etc.)
  4. Major cities in EST zone — Top 10 by population: NYC, Toronto, Havana, Atlanta, Miami, Boston, Philadelphia, Montreal, Jacksonville, Tampa (live clocks for each)
  5. EST vs EDT (150 words) — EST is winter, EDT is summer. Same zone, just different offset. "Spring forward" March → EDT (UTC-4). "Fall back" November → EST (UTC-5).
  6. EST to other time zones — table: EST → CST (1h diff), MST (2h), PST (3h), AKST (4h), HST (5h), UTC (5h ahead), GMT (5h ahead), CET (6h ahead)
  7. DST in 2026 — year strip + countdown to next change + table of past 5 + next 5
  8. Current time in major cities — 5 clocks, all live
  9. Related articles — "DST 2026: Your Guide" + "History of DST" + "About EDT" + "About CST" etc.
  10. FAQ (4-5 questions)
    • What does EST stand for?
    • Is EST the same as New York time?
    • What is the difference between EST and EDT?
    • Which countries use EST?
    • When does EST change to EDT in 2026?

Each of the 11 articles follows this template with abbreviation-specific details.
