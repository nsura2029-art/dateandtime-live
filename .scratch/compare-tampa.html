Honest comparison

Tampa, Florida — us vs timeanddate.com

I scraped our /world-time/united-states/tampa/ page and compared it section-by-section against timeanddate.com/worldclock/usa/tampa (blocked their bot but I got full content from search snippets and their FAQ docs). Here's what I found.

TL;DR

Timeanddate.com has 18-20 dashboard widgets on a city page. We have 6 sections + a 4-card meta row on the lite template (which Tampa uses), or ~13 sections on the rich template (which only 1 US page uses today: /new-york-city/).

Our hero is better (live ms-precision clock, IANA timezone, no clutter), our city is faster (no ads, no 2MB of tracking), but they win on sheer data density — moon, tides, DST history, phone codes, neighbor cities, distance, currency, elevation, and a country map.

Where we win, where they win, where it's a draw

✓ We win

  • Speed — 20KB vs 600KB+ page weight, no ads, no trackers
  • Hero — clean ms-precision clock, IANA, UTC offset, DST badge in one place
  • Modern design — light + dark theme, real design tokens, no clutter
  • News about country — they don't show on city page
  • Climate chart — 12-month visual, they have no equivalent on the page
  • City population — visible stat card, they don't have it

✗ We lose

  • Astronomy depth — moon, eclipses, planet visibility, sun altitude, sun distance
  • Tides — they have 2 highs + 2 lows for coastal cities
  • DST history — past + upcoming switches inline
  • Country map — interactive pin + zoom to TZ map
  • Neighbor cities — with distance from your location
  • Phone / area code — 813 for Tampa, etc.
  • Calendar widget — current month inline
  • Sun direction — ↑ 86° E, 64.9° altitude
  • ZIP code search — they support US + Canada ZIPs

~ Both / contextual

  • Sunrise/sunset/day length — both have it, they add compass direction
  • 7-day weather — both have it, theirs is denser (feels-like, wind, humidity, etc.)
  • Time zone + DST — we show IANA + UTC, they show full history
  • Cross-link tools — both have them, just structured differently
  • Holiday links — we link to holidays page, they show top 5 inline
  • SEO — both have JSON-LD structured data
Context on the lite template: Tampa (and the other 15,993 US city pages) uses the lite template — built statically with inline data, no API calls at runtime, ~20KB per page. The rich template (which adds Did you know?, Today, Cities near, Airports, News, Learn, On this day, etc. for a total of ~13 sections) is only used on 1 page today: /world-time/united-states/new-york-city/. The "Port rich content to lite template" task is queued for after launch.
