City page v2 — informative, beautiful, hooky

Reimagining the city page around timeanddate.com's three "Time/General", "Time Zone", and "DST Changes" tabs as the baseline, then pushing further with our design language, more density, and more internal linking to educational + news content.

Quick wins to add right now

1. City search bar (sticky)

The single highest-value addition. timeanddate.com has a city search at the top of every city page. We have one on the world-time hub but not on individual city pages. This lets users pivot to any of the 33,945 cities in 1 keystroke.

Premium design language

What "premium" means in this context — patterns from Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Apple Human Interface Guidelines:

  • Color-coded card categories — TZ (blue), DST (amber), Astronomy (purple), Weather (sky), Tides (teal), Calendar (pink), News (indigo). Each section feels distinct.
  • Real icons (SVG, not emoji) — Lucide or Phosphor outline icons. Consistent stroke width, optical balance.
  • Subtle gradients on top of solid colors — Hero has gradient bg, cards are flat. Sections alternate between bordered and tinted.
  • Animated clock widgets — smooth ticking analog clock, countdown clocks for DST changes, animated moon phase
  • Numbered data viz — sparklines, ring charts for moon phase, vertical bars for climate, year-strip for DST
  • Pull-quote treatment for educational content
  • Hover micro-interactions on every card (lift, color shift, arrow slide)
  • Better typography hierarchy — display, hero, h2, h3, body, small, eyebrow, caption — already in design system
  • Real whitespace — generous padding, section breaks, breathing room

User hooks (engagement loops)

  1. "Set as home" — Personalize the page. Shows "X hours from {Home city}" everywhere. Stored in localStorage.
  2. "Email me when DST changes" — Sign up form, no account needed. Stored in D1, sent via email (post-launch, need email infra).
  3. "Compare with..." — Inline comparison row: 2-3 cities side-by-side, see time diff live.
  4. "Tell us what's missing" — Feedback widget per city. (Already have feedback_votes in D1.)
  5. "Share this page" — Copy link + Twitter share with pre-filled text + iMessage/SMS on mobile
  6. "Save for later" — Bookmark icon. Could be a personalized dashboard later.
  7. "Plan a meeting here" — Pre-filled meeting planner with this city as one side
  8. "Travel to {City}" — Jet-lag helper: "If you arrive at 9pm local, your body thinks it's..." (home city stored in localStorage)
